# AgentMine

AgentMine is an evidence-first market research project to identify data services that AI agents and their builders will pay for.

## North star
Find a legally usable, frequently updated dataset where developers have a costly recurring information gap. Validate demand before building a paid API or purchasing dedicated hardware.

## Research lanes
1. Agent payment infrastructure and x402 discovery
2. Financial filings and material company events
3. Public tenders and eligibility matching
4. Regulatory change feeds
5. Supplier and business-risk signals

## Evidence rules
- Catalog listings measure supply, not demand.
- Blockchain transactions cannot automatically be attributed to API endpoints or unique customers.
- Mark hypotheses, verified observations, and paid conversions separately.
- Capture source URLs, timestamps, licensing and provenance.
- No personal information, credentials or private data in commits.

## Phase 1 success criteria
- Inventory at least 50 real, deduplicated API resources.
- Identify data provenance and terms for top opportunities.
- Compare at least three promising niches.
- Obtain direct evidence of willingness to pay before significant infrastructure spend.
