# Agent Exchange hypothesis — 2026-10-08

## Idea
An agent-native exchange matching autonomous buyers and service providers, with discovery, quotes, routing, payment, receipts, verification, reputation and dispute workflows. Monetization options: take rate, seller subscriptions, paid verification, enterprise routing/controls. This is a **service marketplace**, not a regulated securities exchange; financial instruments require separate legal analysis.

## Market signals (not validation)
- AP2 protocol: https://ap2-protocol.org/ and https://github.com/google-agentic-commerce/AP2 (agent authorization and payment evidence)
- Agent402: https://marketplace.agent402.app/ (claims marketplace with x402 and trust scoring)
- PayanAgent: https://payanagent.com/ (claims aggregated x402 catalog, zero platform fee)
- Atelier: https://useatelier.ai/x402 (claims agent marketplace and take rate)
- Inference Exchange: https://ethglobal.com/showcase/inference-exchange-6awz7 (hackathon quote-based inference market)

These are providers' claims, not independently verified revenues, transaction counts or traction. Competition already exists; generic listings are unlikely to be enough.

## Potential defensible wedges
1. Outcome verification and dispute evidence for paid agent tasks.
2. Routing to cheapest reliable provider with uptime and quality guarantees.
3. Enterprise permission/budget layer for autonomous agent procurement.
4. Vertical B2B exchange where buyer and supplier acquisition can be concentrated.
5. White-label exchange / settlement / reporting for existing ecosystems.

## Risks
Cold-start two-sided market, distribution, platform fee compression, wash transactions, quality fraud, identity and security, regulatory compliance, refund handling, costs and inability to prove repeat purchasing.

## Validation before building an exchange
- Inventory 10 direct/adjacent competitors and documented fees.
- Identify one narrow repeatable task with genuine buyers and sellers.
- Obtain buyer commitment to route at least one paid task and seller commitment to fulfill.
- Simulate matching/verification manually first; measure contribution margin and trust failures.
- Only build infrastructure if this narrow wedge works.

## Decision
Research as a parallel hypothesis to DaaS, not an immediate pivot. Compare on OPPORTUNITY_SCORING.md and keep evidence separate from conjecture.
