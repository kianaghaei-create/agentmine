# North star — profitability over technology

**Goal:** Discover, validate and operate a profitable, scalable, low-maintenance internet business. Seek revenue that can run in the background, with realistic monitoring and maintenance. Explore surprising, under-served opportunities.

DaaS, x402, agent tooling, procurement, financial feeds and market intelligence are **candidate approaches**, not sacred commitments. Pivot, narrow or abandon any thesis based on buyer evidence.

## Evaluation criteria
1. Verified willingness to pay, repeat usage and retention.
2. Attractive unit economics after acquisition, data, payment, infrastructure, support and compliance costs.
3. Legally reliable data rights and provenance.
4. Differentiation and distribution advantage.
5. Automation potential and operational resilience.
6. Small upfront cost and short time to first sale.

## Guardrails
- No fabricated demand, revenue or usage metrics.
- No credentials in git; respect terms, privacy and rate limits.
- No spending, public monetized deployments or major risk increases without owner approval.
- Do not optimize for API calls or GitHub activity at the expense of cash flow.
