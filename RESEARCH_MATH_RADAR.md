# Mathematical opportunities — research hypotheses

Online matching: choose providers as requests and supply arrive over time; benchmark against simple cheapest-first routing.

Mechanism design: make offer ranking robust against strategic supplier misreporting; test only after observing competing suppliers.

Optimal transport and min-cost flow: distribute many tasks across limited provider capacity; start with a simple optimization baseline.

Bayesian reliability estimation: avoid selecting providers based on a handful of successes; calibrate uncertainty and update after outcomes.

Sequential decision making and contextual bandits: explore unfamiliar suppliers while minimizing failed purchases, subject to explicit spending controls.

Verifiable delivery: hash content, preserve provenance and check schemas; payment proof does not imply data truth.

Every mathematical idea needs a measured operational bottleneck and a baseline comparison before implementation. No assumption that a recently published theorem automatically creates market demand.
