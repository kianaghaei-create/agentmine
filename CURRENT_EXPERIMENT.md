# Current experiment — Find the first profitable wedge

Status: **in progress; no validated demand yet**
Started: 2026-10-08
Owner: AgentMine

## Hypothesis
There is a repeatable, legally usable data or agent-workflow niche with customers who will pay more than the cost of acquisition and delivery.

## Method
1. Collect real market supply data from authorized public sources, retaining provenance.
2. Assess demand evidence separately: observed calls, unique payers if trustworthy, reviews, adoption, developer complaints and buyer interviews.
3. Score opportunity: urgency, frequency, ability to pay, cost of data, legal rights, distribution, competition and automation potential.
4. Create a tiny demonstration and test a paid pilot before investing in hosting hardware.

## Minimum evidence
- 50+ deduplicated real listings (supply indicator only).
- 3 shortlisted opportunities with documented competitors and source terms.
- 5 buyer conversations or direct purchase/paid pilot signals.
- Basic unit-economics model and an explicit go / pivot / stop decision.

## Falsification / pivot triggers
- Listings show supply but no credible willingness to pay.
- Required data cannot be legally or reliably acquired.
- Competitor economics or acquisition costs make margins unattractive.
- Another adjacent opportunity has better buyer evidence and faster path to cash.
